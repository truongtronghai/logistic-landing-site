Safety & maintenance

Inspections are a habit, not an event.

  • Every tractor gets a daily pre-trip and a documented 90-day PM in our shop.
  • Twin cameras in every cab — a safety record we show, not just claim.
  • Drivers on relay and split schedules for lanes over 300 miles.
  • Real-time ELD data reviewed weekly for speed, stops, and duty hours.
